THE SPACE
BETWEEN.

Yin Yang Gallery is a Johannesburg-born visual studio founded by photographer Tumeleng Shiri. We chase the tension between soft and sharp, quiet and loud, raw and refined.

Every frame is treated as a small universe: intentional composition, honest emotion and a little bit of play. The result is imagery that feels editorial without losing its human pulse.
